What is a Smart City?

A Smart City is an urban area that uses technology, data and innovative solutions to improve public services and the quality of life of citizens.

Smart City development focuses on transportation, water management, waste management, energy, environment, public safety and digital services.

The main objective is to create a city that is sustainable, connected, efficient and citizen-friendly.
