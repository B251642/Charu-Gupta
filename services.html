About Smart City


A Smart City uses modern technology to improve the quality of life for citizens. It connects transportation, healthcare, education, security, water supply, electricity and communication through digital systems.



Vision

To create a clean, safe, sustainable and digitally connected city.

Mission

Improve public services using technology and provide better living standards.

Objectives

• Smart Transportation
• Smart Healthcare
• Digital Education
• Green Environment

Benefits

Cleaner city, less pollution, better traffic management, public safety and efficient services.
